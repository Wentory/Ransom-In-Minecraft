# Ransom In Minecraft

## About

This mod brings Ransom (A-90) from DOORS into Minecraft.

By default, Ransom randomly appears every 180-360 seconds. When he appears, the player must stop moving. If you move or press anything while the STOP sign is active, you will be jumpscared and the challenge will begin. Your goal is to recover coins from infected blocks.

To collect coins, hold right-click on an infected block or break it.

The challenge takes place inside a limited area with a default radius of 64 blocks. If you leave it, Ransom will get angry, yell at you, generate a new infection zone, and add 30 coins to your debt.

And do not try to escape the challenge. Ransom will not be pleased.

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LSPLASH. Ransom, DOORS, and the original Ransom assets belong to LSPLASH.

[Play DOORS on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/6516141723/DOORS)

## Warning

This mod contains jumpscares, sudden loud sounds, screen shake, and flashing red and white effects.

## Config

Open **Mods → Ransom In Minecraft → Config**.

You can change several parts of Ransom's challenge:

- The vertical infection spawn range
- The radius of the challenge area
- Ransom's minimum and maximum spawn interval
- Whether hotbar items are deleted after a failed challenge
